What is AI Store?

AI Store is an AI discovery and learning app developed by LINS IO. Its goal is simple: make the growing AI ecosystem easier to explore.

Instead of searching separately for an AI image generator, coding assistant, writing tool, productivity app, prompt resource or AI-related learning opportunity, AI Store brings different parts of the ecosystem together in a single experience.

The platform currently features more than 1000 AI tools across different categories, making it easier to discover tools based on what you actually want to do.

Why an AI discovery app is useful

The problem with AI today isn't a lack of tools. It is the opposite. There are too many of them.

Every week brings new AI products, updates and platforms. Searching for the right tool can quickly become a task of its own.

AI Store focuses on reducing that friction by organizing useful AI resources into a simpler discovery experience.

2. Generate Better AI Prompts

Getting useful results from AI often depends on how clearly you communicate your goal.

AI Store includes a Prompt Generator designed to help users create prompts faster. Instead of starting from an empty input field, users can generate a structured prompt and adapt it to their needs.

3. Save Prompts with Prompt Vault

Once you find or create a prompt that works well, the next problem is remembering it.

Prompt Vault gives users a dedicated place to store and manage useful prompts.

Less noise. More useful information.
