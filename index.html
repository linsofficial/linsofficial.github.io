FAQ

Common questions.

Are LINS IO apps free to use?
Yes. PixelRush, Scan & Explain, AI Store, and our web-based Resume Builder are all free to download and use.
Does PixelRush work completely offline?
Yes. All 12 mini games inside PixelRush (formerly LinsPlay) run locally on your device with zero Wi-Fi or mobile data required.
What happened to LinsCam AI and LinsPlay?
Both apps received major upgrades and clearer names: LinsPlay is now PixelRush (expanded to 12 games), and LinsCam AI is now Scan & Explain.
