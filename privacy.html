Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LINS IO ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you visit our official website at linsio.site.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies specifically to the LINS IO website, product showcase pages, and blog articles hosted on linsio.site. Individual mobile apps and web tools published by LINS IO (such as PixelRush, Scan & Explain, AI Store, and Resume Builder) may maintain their own product-specific privacy policies tailored to their features.

2. Information We Collect

The LINS IO website is designed as a lightweight, informational brand and product showcase:

  • No User Accounts: We do not require registration, logins, or personal profiles to browse linsio.site.
  • No Intentional Personal Data Collection: We do not directly collect, sell, or rent personal information such as your name, address, or phone number through website browsing.
  • Direct Communications: If you email us directly at linsio.official@gmail.com, we only use your email address and message content to respond to your inquiry or support request.

3. Hosting, Logs & Third-Party Links

Our website is hosted via static web infrastructure (GitHub Pages). Standard server or network logs (such as IP address, browser type, or basic request metadata) may be processed automatically by the hosting provider for security and reliability.

Pages on linsio.site contain links to external platforms—including Google Play Store, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Telegram, WhatsApp, and third-party AI resources referenced in our blog. Once you leave linsio.site, those third-party platforms are governed by their own privacy policies.

4. Product-Specific Policies

For details about data handling inside our individual products (such as offline local storage, camera permissions, or Google AdMob advertising where applicable), please review each product's dedicated privacy policy linked on Google Play or on its respective product page.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 through linsio.site. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us via email, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as LINS IO grows and introduces new products. Any updates will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
